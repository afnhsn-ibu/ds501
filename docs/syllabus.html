Syllabus

DS 501 is a graduate (master's) course at International Balkan University, Skopje. Students arrive from computer science, engineering, economics, business and the social sciences, so the course assumes no prior data science and builds the programming alongside the ideas.

One session of about three hours each week: three 45-minute lessons and a practice.

How a week runs

  • Three lessons of 45 minutes, each with a scenario worked in pairs and one Check Your Understanding question.
  • One practice of 45–60 minutes in class, finished as homework.
  • A quiz of 15 multiple-choice questions, plus one short applied question from Week 2 on.
  • Two or three required papers, with a half-page note due at the next class.

Using AI tools

You may use an AI assistant to explain concepts, debug your own code and improve your writing. You must be able to explain every line you submit. Work you cannot explain is treated as work you did not do.

State in your submission which tool you used and what for. That statement costs you no marks. Omitting it does. Week 11 treats reviewing AI-written code as a skill in its own right, including the failure modes to look for.

Companion texts

  • McKinney, Python for Data Analysis, 3rd ed. (2022) — weeks 3–5
  • James, Witten, Hastie, Tibshirani & Taylor, An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in Python (2023) — weeks 6–10
  • Provost & Fawcett, Data Science for Business (2013) — weeks 1–2 and 9

None is required to buy. The relevant chapters are listed on each week’s page.

Tools

Python 3 in Google Colab, pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, Plotly, SciPy, statsmodels, scikit-learn, SQLite, PySpark. Every notebook opens in Colab with no local setup.
