Course project

You work on one dataset and one question for the whole semester and present the result in Week 12. The project is individual. The point is not a high score on a benchmark; it is a defensible answer to a real question, with the uncertainty stated honestly.

Choosing a question

Good questions here are small, local and answerable. Skopje air quality, a North Macedonian open dataset, public transport, or something from your own field of work. A question that needs data you cannot get is not a project.

Common mistake: choosing the dataset first and then hunting for a question inside it.
